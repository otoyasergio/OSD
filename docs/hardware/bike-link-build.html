Two boxes. A sketch of the hardware, what the finished boxes look like, and what to buy first.

Bike box · build this first

Under the seat

One box on one motorcycle. It wakes when the key turns on, asks the bike for kilometers and fault codes, then goes back to sleep. After the bike has been sitting, it measures the resting battery voltage and keeps that reading until a phone or the shop tablet is next to it.

The first handoff is Bluetooth. Staff read the reading on the phone and type it into the motorcycle page. Power is a fused wire on the battery. The 16-pin plug does not power the box. This first cable is for a Triumph that still has the regular 16-pin OBD socket under the seat.

Mailbox · later

In a window

A listener with wall power and the building’s Wi-Fi. It uses the same short-range radio as the bike box, once that radio is fitted. At the shop it sits in a plastic box in a window at 99 River Street that faces where the bikes park. Not a metal electrical room. Not a closed cabinet.

A phone is the first handoff. The mailbox is the second. Bike-to-bike relay, and coverage across Toronto, are out of scope.

No cell. No satellite.

No cellular modem, no SIM card, and no monthly radio bill. No satellite radio and no satellite fee. Customer texts stay on the Wix Inbox thread the shop already uses. Nothing in this build sends a text.

Build the Bluetooth box first. Leave the radio off until that box sleeps, reads a bike, and a person can copy the reading into the motorcycle page.

Wiring

First build, Bluetooth only, for a Triumph with the regular 16-pin socket. The radio header stays empty. The fuse is within 15 cm of the battery positive post. The 16-pin plug is signal only.

Male SAE J1962 plug. The Triumph already has the female socket under the seat. The pigtail is the signal cable. The 18 AWG battery leads do not go through this plug. K-line is not this build. A 2006–2012 triple can wear this same plug and still speak K-line on pin 7. Leave that bike alone. Do not plug this into the small red 6-pin on a newer Triumph.

Pin What the plug is for What this cable does
4 or 5 Ground. Use the pin that actually reaches battery negative. Signal ground, 22 AWG, to the box
6 CAN H Twisted pair to SN65HVD230 pin 7
14 CAN L Twisted pair to SN65HVD230 pin 6
16 Battery positive. On some Triumph triples it stays live with the key off. Sense only if it follows the key. Does not power the box. Otherwise leave it open.
7, and the rest K-line, and pins this cable does not use Cut those wires off and insulate them

Before you trust the pigtail, buzz each wire to its pin. Battery negative disconnected: pin 4 or pin 5 reads through to battery negative, pin 16 is near 0 V with the key off and about battery voltage with the key on, and pins 6 and 14 are not shorted to ground or to battery positive. If pin 16 stays hot with the key off, leave it open and move the sense wire to a circuit that really follows the key, such as the tail lamp.

Pass mark and what it stores

Under 50 microamps

Measure in series at the fuse. Key off. USB unplugged. CAN power off. Wait one minute after the box goes to sleep. Over 50 microamps, it stays on the bench. Do not put it on a customer bike.

On a small 8 amp-hour sport-bike battery, 50 microamps all winter is about 3 percent. A box at 1 milliamp is not acceptable.

Three stored values

  1. Kilometers. The whole number already on that motorcycle, in kilometres or miles. The box does not convert. If the bike does not answer, store a blank. Never send 0 to mean “could not read it.”
  2. Resting battery voltage. One decimal, about 20 minutes after the key is off. A reading while the engine is running is not stored. If there is no rested sample, leave the voltage blank. The box does not call the battery good or flat. A lithium battery rests higher than a lead-acid battery. Send the measured number anyway.
  3. Fault codes. Short codes such as P0335 or C14, up to 20. Store them. Do not clear them.

A reading needs at least one of those three. Staff type it into the motorcycle page. The sticker and the Bluetooth name are TM-BL- plus four digits.

What the box does not do

  • It does not decide that a service is due. The shop app does that from the kilometers and the date.
  • It does not clear codes, and it does not send a clear-codes command.
  • It does not text the customer. Customer texts stay on Wix Inbox.
