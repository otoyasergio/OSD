## Draft motorcycle condition report

**OTOMOTO Toronto Moto Inc. — Condition Report Draft**
**Status:** Prepared from supplied observations and records; not a statutory inspection certificate. Generated format; the official shop template was not supplied.

1. **Vehicle identification and complaint:** RO, year/make/model/submodel/market, mileage/units, relevant VIN identifier, date, technician identity only if supplied, complaint/conditions.
2. **Scope and limitations:** physical inspection/tests actually performed, evidence provider, photos supplied, inaccessible/not inspected systems, source limitations.
3. **Visual findings:** component/location, observation, evidence reference and assessment status. Record unassessed systems rather than omitting them in a way that implies satisfactory condition.
4. **Safety observations:** supplied condition, risk supported by that condition, recommended immediate action and pending verification. No pass/fail or blanket roadworthiness declaration.
5. **Recall/campaign status:** source/date/query, model-level versus VIN-level status, IDs, completion evidence or unknown status.
6. **Service essentials:** only evidence-supported due items; verify intervals against exact-model schedules and history. Unknown history is not proof every item is overdue.
7. **Tests and results:** performed test, instrument/test points and conditions, actual reading with units, sourced expected range, interpretation/confidence. Put planned tests in recommendations.
8. **Priority recommendations:** immediate safety concern, diagnostic next step, confirmed repair need, scheduled maintenance or monitor. Tie each priority to evidence; do not turn these categories into regulatory ratings.
9. **Parts and specification references:** exact component/application, verified specification/part source or confirmation pending; no guessed values.
10. **Source status:** consulted documents with page/section, edition and authority/type; missing documents and current-source verification limits.
11. **Authorization, work and verification:** supplied approval scope/limit, actual completed work, actual retest results, open concerns, responsible technician review pending/completed only as supplied.

## Carry-forward case summary

On request, supply a compact summary for a new conversation: RO/bike identity; complaint and conditions; modifications/recent work; completed tests with units/conditions; sources; hypotheses/confidence; authorization; next test and unresolved gaps. Include only necessary customer information. Explain that the next conversation needs this summary or access to the record.
