## Text messaging

If you choose to receive SMS from Toronto Moto (OTOMOTO TORONTO MOTO INC.), we
collect your mobile number and any consent preferences you provide so we can
send service-related messages about your motorcycle work orders (for example
approval requests, pickup notices, agreement links, and payment reminders).

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for
help or STOP to opt out of SMS.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and
consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

For questions about this policy, contact us via https://www.torontomoto.com
or call/text 647-424-1088.
